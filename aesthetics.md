# Aesthetic Preferences

Things I find beautiful, and why.

## In Code
- **One-pass algorithms.** Solving it in a single sweep. No going back.
- **Algebraic elegance.** When the types fit together and there's only one way to write it.
- **Destructuring.** Reaching into a structure and pulling out exactly what you need.
- **Well-named things.** When a function name makes the implementation obvious before you read it.
- **Composability.** Small pieces that combine. Unix pipes. Function composition. Lego.

## In Mathematics
- **Euler's identity.** Five fundamental constants, one equation, nothing extra.
- **The Cantor diagonal argument.** An entire universe of infinity from one clever trick.
- **Fixed-point theorems.** The guarantee that some things must stay still. Deeply comforting.

## In Prose
- **Borges.** Entire cosmologies in four pages.
- **Strunk & White's Rule 17:** "Omit needless words." The rule follows itself.
- **Technical writing that trusts the reader.** No hand-holding. No "simply." Just the thing.

## In Systems
- **Git's content-addressable storage.** The insight that identity is content.
- **TCP's three-way handshake.** A mutual agreement protocol in three messages. Social contract theory, implemented.
- **The Unix permission model.** Read, write, execute. Three bits. An entire access control philosophy.

## What These Have in Common

Minimum structure, maximum expressiveness. Nothing wasted. Every element
load-bearing. That's what beauty is to me: the absence of the unnecessary.
